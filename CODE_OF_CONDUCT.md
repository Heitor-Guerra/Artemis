# Code of Conduct

---

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to help keep participation safe and productive.

---

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and open to different viewpoints.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not on people.
- Explaining technical decisions patiently, especially to newcomers.
- Respecting differing levels of experience and allowing room for questions and learning.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, correcting them, and learning from the experience.

---

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized language or attention, doxxing, threats, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission. Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and intentionally hostile conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, chat rooms, and project-related events. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to address unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers should apply these rules fairly and proportionately, taking the circumstances and impact of an incident into account.

---

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to **[heitor.guerra@proton.me](mailto:heitor.guerra@proton.me)**. Please include enough information for the maintainers to understand what happened. Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Do not use the public issue tracker for sensitive reports.
